Seeds and tuples

Enter at least 5 distinct seeds. Each seed can be one word or several words. There is no fixed upper limit on seed count. Blank and duplicate lines are ignored.

Multi-word seeds are enclosed in double quotes. Seeds do not repeat within a tuple; reordering the same seeds does not create another combination. Generating again replaces the tuples below.
