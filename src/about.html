Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026.

BootCaT builds text corpora locally in your browser. There is no BootCaT account, server, analytics, advertising or telemetry. Project names, language settings, seeds, tuples, search records, tracked project tabs, URLs, downloaded documents and extracted texts are stored in this browser profile. They are not sent to the developer.

Requests to other websites

Opening a search sends the generated query, including any file-type or site restrictions, to the search engine you select. Visiting or downloading a document sends its URL and normal network request information, including your IP address, to the destination website and any redirect destinations. These websites have their own privacy policies.

Search pages opened in browser tabs use your normal browser session. When you request recovery of a PDF from the current page, BootCaT can retry the download using your session for that website; the browser may send its cookies to that site. Ordinary batch downloads omit credentials. BootCaT does not send cookies to the developer.

Collecting search results and adding a page to the corpus read the relevant page in your browser. Content extraction, language detection, manual file imports and corpus export run locally. BootCaT does not upload your corpus or imported files to an external processing service. HTTP source URLs use an unencrypted connection; HTTPS is preferable.

Storage and deletion

BootCaT does not synchronize projects between devices. A new project clears the current project's saved data; excluded-site defaults can be saved separately and retained across projects. Removing a document deletes its saved files and text from BootCaT. Exported files are saved to a location you choose and are not deleted by clearing a project. Removing the extension normally removes its browser-managed storage.

Permissions

Website access allows downloading documents from the URLs you choose and reading relevant tabs for search collection or page capture. Tab access tracks searches opened for your project, shows their status, and closes them when you request collection. Local storage retains your project and preferences. BootCaT does not monitor unrelated browsing in order to build a browsing history.

Contact

For privacy questions and support, use the publisher contact details on BootCaT's Mozilla Add-ons or Chrome Web Store listing.
